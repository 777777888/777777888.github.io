Cariboo Yarns Expeditions

An independent brand in development: rugged performance wear with a street silhouette, built for exploration work in the backcountry. This 1-of-1 jacket was patterned and sewn entirely by hand as the flagship prototype — construction quality benchmarked against Arc'teryx Veilance.

The woven neck label is adapted from an original painting of a peak in the Omineca Range, an extremely remote part of northern-central British Columbia.
