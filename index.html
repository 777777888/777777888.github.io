Cariboo Yarns Exploration

An independent brand in development: rugged performance wear with a street silhouette, built for exploration work in the backcountry. The flagship piece is a 1-of-1 jacket, patterned and sewn entirely by hand — construction quality benchmarked against Arc'teryx Veilance.

The woven neck label is adapted from an original painting of a remote peak in northern-central British Columbia.
